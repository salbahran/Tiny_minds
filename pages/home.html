Overview

Playful learning for curious little ones!

Tiny Brains helps children aged 2–6 explore letters, numbers and animals through fun games, stories and interactive activities.

Who is it for?
⌄

Designed specifically for curious toddlers and preschoolers aged 2 to 6 years old.

What will children learn?
⌄

Children explore the alphabet, animal sounds, counting numbers, and basic word building.

How does it work?
⌄

Through fun, safe, interactive mini-games and engaging visuals tailored for young minds.
